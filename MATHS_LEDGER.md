## Audit notes

- Capture and validation are deliberately separate. A checked row may still be `CAPTURED`, `NEEDS_VALIDATION`, `READY_FOR_EXPERIMENT`, `REJECTED`, or `SUPERSEDED`.
- Where the conversation named a technique but did not give an equation, the ledger says **FORMULA NOT YET SPECIFIED** rather than inventing one.
- The conventional/literal Avellaneda–Stoikov control law is **not** promoted as a production formula. Its useful ideas (reservation price, inventory control, fill-intensity optimization) survive, while binary-CARA/state-payoff models are the preferred prediction-market-native formulations.
- Midpoint-only arbitrage, fixed-cent spread policy, independent per-market inventory risk, and raw-volume-as-quality are treated as ideas to avoid rather than active mathematical components.

## Corrections / supersessions discovered in the audit

1. **Literal Avellaneda–Stoikov is not the production model.** The earlier A-S family remains useful research context, but its Brownian mid-price / stationary Poisson-fill structure is inappropriate as a literal prediction-market assumption. The active challengers are exact binary CARA, multi-state CARA, empirical fill hazards, and fill-conditioned adverse-selection models.
2. **Fixed-cent spread logic is superseded as a universal mathematical policy.** The research moved toward log-odds-space quoting because equal cent moves have radically different informational meaning near 0/1 versus 0.5.
3. **Independent per-market inventory skew is superseded as the preferred risk model.** Portfolio/state risk and the approximation `r≈p-γΣq` capture correlated election exposure.
4. **Midpoint probability violations are not arbitrage.** The canonical arb test uses executable bid/ask/depth and a payoff-state proof.
5. **Raw external-market volume is not a sufficient quality weight.** External-FV weighting should focus on executable depth, spread, impact, freshness, mapping quality and actual predictive performance.
6. **A generic toxicity score is not the target.** The preferred empirical label is fill-conditioned future information markout.
7. **Clock-time volatility alone is not trusted in thin markets.** Activity/event-time support needs to be accounted for before event-risk regime changes.
