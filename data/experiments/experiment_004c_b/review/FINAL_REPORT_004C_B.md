The hard PRE Colombia presidential subset is statistically unusual under the time null, but its controlled beta is only 0.0086 versus the frozen 0.10 economically meaningful threshold. ## Robustness and heterogeneity

The surviving soft Colombia family remains significant at 60 seconds (beta 0.06723, q 0.009990, membership p 0.000999) and 120 seconds (beta 0.09116, q 0.009990, membership p 0.000999). At 300 seconds the same family no longer survives the frozen robustness FDR family (beta 0.06077, p 0.016983, q 0.169830).

This longer-horizon behaviour is supporting robustness only. It is not used to rescue the 30-second primary result.

Member-level controlled betas inside the surviving three-member family are heterogeneous: approximately -0.00025, 0.12065 and 0.05557. The family-level effect therefore should not be described as a universal response by every member. Two members carry the positive effect while one is effectively flat.

The comparable Peru discovery soft families do not replicate the result. The House most-seats family has a positive 30-second beta but fails both 1% FDR and matched-membership specificity; the Senate family is weaker and also fails. This limits the evidence to a challenge-family phenomenon rather than a general cross-election competitive-family law.

## Hard-family diagnostics

No full exhaustive family is empirically complete under the frozen 004A.2 usability rule. Consequently there are zero valid historical `sum(p)=1` tests and zero executable full-basket certificates.

The partial Colombia presidential PRE family produces many `sum(p)>1` midpoint violations, but the membership null shows that the resulting correction statistic is not specific to the true economic family. This is consistent with generic overround/staleness/common-market effects being sufficient to create apparent partial-family inconsistency.

Several Peru hard scopes have either no complete family-state observations or no identifiable structural signal after the frozen controls. The experiment therefore provides no promotable evidence for a hard structural correction mechanism.

## Executability

Every proven exhaustive parent family is missing at least one frozen usable leg in every historical primary scope. The executable-basket lane therefore fails closed. Midpoint inconsistency is not labelled arbitrage, and no profitability claim is made.
## Interpretation

The battery rejects the strongest version of the mechanism: historical partial mechanical-family inconsistency does not show a specific, replicated correction process strong enough to satisfy the frozen structural gate.

It does leave one narrower signal: short-horizon leave-target-out movement within a small economically competitive family contains incremental information for subsequent family-member movement in one challenge PRE window. The effect survives the strict 1% FDR family and a matched-membership null, with supportive 60s and 120s robustness.

Because the analogous Peru discovery families fail and the surviving family's member effects are heterogeneous, this should be treated as a **candidate soft competitive redistribution mechanism**, not as established generic alpha. Any follow-up should be separately preregistered and should test the same leave-target-out construction on additional complete competitive families without changing the current 004C-B disposition.
