### What timestamps can establish

**A. Local receipt→decision causality — conditionally possible.** If a legally permitted, authenticated Virginia-result response is captured **prospectively**, preserve original bytes/SHA-256, exact source/contest/revision IDs, response headers, trusted UTC time and **same-process monotonic receipt**. A later PAPER decision in that monotonic domain can prove only that *these bytes were locally known by receipt, before that decision*. Capture process/host, clock sync/epoch and append-only correction identity. Different hosts' monotonic counters cannot be compared without independently demonstrated alignment and error bound.

**B. Publisher→receipt speed — UNMEASURED.** Jan-2026 archive CSV row `Timestamp` is timezone-naive **edit time**; `lastUpdated`, `asOf`, export `createdAt`, HTTP `Last-Modified` and October retrieval are different events. None attests first public release of an individual revision. Never backdate a local receipt or label a page-update time `published_at`. Local receipt causality **does not** prove government-to-SIG speed alpha.
