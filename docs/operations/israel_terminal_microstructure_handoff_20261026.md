## 2. Executable opportunity / after-cost capture

For every timestamped price-acceleration alert (>=3pp/1min or >=6pp/5min), replay a hypothetical *two-sided liquidity-taking round trip* against the **recorded** Polymarket L2 sweeps at 50, 250 and 1000 shares. Model entry at the first observed book snapshot after signal + assumed **1s / 5s / 30s latency**, followed by exit after **60s / 300s** at the other side's executable VWAP. Require a fresh book and a matching sample within 2s of the simulated time. An observed quote at 15s cannot be misrepresented as a 1s execution.

- Assume 50 bps per entry/exit leg and an extra 0.5 cents/share uncertainty haircut by default; these are **illustrative**, NOT verified Polymarket fees or SIG fees. The public CLOB fee-rate endpoint returned HTTP 403 from East on 26 Oct.
- Execution costs include actual displayed top-level depth and price impact **at sampled timestamps**, assumed fees and latency. They **do not** include maker queue position, fleeting-quote survival, order acknowledgements, actual private liquidity, exchange halts, independent settlement fair value, or SIG order execution.
- Quantity that cannot sweep **both** entrance and exit sides at the chosen size is not counted as a replay. Polymarket USDC-model P&L is not SIG SUSQies or a trading profit.
- Positive replay after a price alert is **hindsight evidence, not predictive alpha**. An alert generated by price itself cannot demonstrate an information advantage.

## 3. Information lag

- Quote timestamp: Polymarket venue's reported timestamp (where present) and East's first receipt timestamp; keep provider-receipt lag separate from source-publication timing.
- Reporting discovery: Google News RSS first-seen time versus item's reported `pubDate`. This measures *syndication/reader delay*, **not** official-election-results latency or independent confirmation. Multiple rebroadcasts of the same wire do not prove independent sources.
- For authenticated or human independently reviewed reporting: `israel_verified_event.py` accepts one explicitly reviewed event with two different source hosts and human-reviewed independent provenance, requiring exact instrument and published UTC. It records publication→observer lag, and the analysis finds the **first observed >=2pp** book-midpoint change after publication (if any within 30m). It does NOT prove causality and does not authenticate the claims itself. Empty when no independently reviewed evidence exists.
- The Israeli Central Elections Committee website/API has not been authenticated or made machine-accessible from East. Do not treat any test-environment election totals as real.
